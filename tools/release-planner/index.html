Release Planner

Pick your release date and get a dated checklist, working backwards from it.

Saved on this device. Your dates and ticked boxes stay in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. The timings are typical, so adjust them to your distributor and plant.

A guide, not a rule. Distributors, stores and pressing plants set their own deadlines and they change, so check yours before you commit. Release dates here are plain calendar dates, so the calendar file creates all day events.
