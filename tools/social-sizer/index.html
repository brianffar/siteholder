Social Sizer

Drop in one image and get every common social size. Drag the image inside each frame to choose the crop, add your logo, then download.

Private by design. Your images are edited right here in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.

Drag an image hereor click to choose one. JPG, PNG or WebP. The bigger the better.
Drag the picture inside a frame to move it. Use the slider under it to zoom. Untick a frame to skip it.

Sizes are the usual ones for each platform and they change from time to time. Stories and reels hide the top and bottom of the frame behind buttons, so the logo stays inside the safe zones there.
