    Against typical streaming targets

    ServiceTypical targetTurned down by

    Services normalize playback to a target loudness and change these settings from time to time. Dance masters are often louder than streaming targets, and that is fine for clubs. A louder track is simply turned down on those services.

    Loudness follows ITU-R BS.1770-4 (K-weighted, gated), tested against reference tones. True peak is estimated with 4x oversampling and can differ from other meters by a fraction of a dB. Compressed files (MP3, AAC) are measured after decoding. This tool gives information, not pass or fail.
