    What we check
    • Audio format: WAV or AIFF is the safe choice. FLAC is accepted by some stores. MP3 is flagged because most stores want lossless masters.
    • Bit depth and sample rate: 16 or 24 bit at 44.1, 48, 88.2 or 96 kHz is typical. 32-bit float and odd sample rates are flagged.
    • Levels (optional): true peak above 0 dBTP, clipped samples, silence at the start or end, and files that are really mono.
    • Artwork: square, JPG or PNG, at least 1400 px a side (3000 px is the usual distributor spec), RGB colour, under 10 MB.
    • Whole release: missing artwork, duplicate track numbers, and mixed sample rates or bit depths.

    Store and distributor requirements change and differ. Treat these as a first pass and check the current spec from your distributor before you submit. The zip puts your renamed files in a folder with a short report.
