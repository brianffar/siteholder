How it would work

  1. Relaunch email first. It goes to the old promo list, asks who wants to keep getting promos, and clears out dead addresses.
  2. One campaign per release. Artwork, tracks and info come straight from the catalog.
  3. Every recipient gets their own link. No login. Tracks stream first.
  4. Feedback unlocks downloads. WAV and MP3 links expire after 7 days.
  5. Feedback comes back to the dashboard, ready for social posts and "supported by" lists.
